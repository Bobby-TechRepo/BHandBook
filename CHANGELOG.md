# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (first release candidate)

* Handwritten maths recognition on the device (CoMER, with a built-in fallback recogniser), arithmetic with exact fractions, columns, equations, simultaneous equations.
* Graphs with pan, zoom and trace; named values and functions; angle, result and money modes.
* Answers and steps in the Handlee font; the answer is hidden while its steps are shown, and the other way round.
* Select, move, resize and delete several expressions at once (one undo step); search across canvases; "Show all".
* Image and PDF export; backup and restore of canvases as files.
* Dark theme now also darkens the canvas; icons follow the theme.
* Release build: R8 shrinking with ONNX Runtime keep rules, arm64 / arm32 only, signing from `keystore.properties`, privacy-safe backup rules.
