# Store listing draft (Google Play)

## Title (30 characters)
Infinite Canvas Calculator

## Short description (80 characters)
Write maths by hand on an endless canvas. Get answers and steps. Works offline.

## Full description
Write a calculation with your finger or a stylus, anywhere on an endless canvas, and the answer appears next to it. No keyboard, no buttons.

* **Arithmetic and algebra.** Write 25+15= or 2x+5=15 and see the answer with the steps, in a handwriting font.
* **Columns, like on paper.** Write numbers one under another and draw a line: the total appears below it.
* **Simultaneous equations.** Write two equations one under the other and they are solved together.
* **Graphs.** Equations in x and y can be plotted. Zoom and move inside the graph, and tap to trace points and intersections.
* **Names and functions.** Write a = 5 or f(x) = x^2 - 4 and use them anywhere else on the canvas; change one and every answer updates.
* **Degrees, radians or gradians; fractions or decimals; money mode** with thousands separators and a currency symbol.
* **Fix a misread in one tap.** Alternative readings are offered when the app is unsure.
* **Several at once.** Select, move, resize and delete expressions together; search every canvas.
* **Share and keep.** Export a canvas as an image or a PDF, or back it up to a file and restore it on another phone.
* **Light and dark themes.**

**Private by design:** it works completely offline, has no network permission, no ads and no account. Your handwriting never leaves your phone.

## Data safety form (Play Console)
* Does the app collect or share any user data? **No.**
* Is all data processed on the device? **Yes.** Data is not encrypted in transit because it is never transmitted.
* Can users request that data be deleted? Data is only on the device: uninstalling deletes it.
