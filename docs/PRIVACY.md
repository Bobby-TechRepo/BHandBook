# Privacy policy: Infinite Canvas Calculator

*Last updated: 12 November 2026.*

**The short version: the app keeps everything on your device and sends nothing anywhere.**

* **No network.** The app does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the internet. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting inside the app.
* **What it stores, on your device only:** your canvases (your handwriting, the expressions read from it, and the answers), your settings, and the position and size of graphs. This stays in the app's private storage.
* **Handwriting recognition runs on your phone.** Your writing is never uploaded for recognition.
* **Optional handwriting samples.** If (and only if) you turn on the option to save handwriting samples to improve recognition, the app saves the strokes of expressions you correct, together with your corrected text, in a folder on your device. It never sends them; they are excluded from Android's backup. You can turn it off and delete the folder at any time; you decide whether to share them with the developer, for example by sending the files yourself.
* **Backup.** Android's automatic backup (if you have it turned on in your phone's settings) may copy your canvases and settings to your Google account, under Google's own policies. Handwriting samples are never included. "Back up" in the Canvases sheet makes a file that you choose where to keep or send.
* **Sharing.** When you share a canvas as an image, a PDF or a backup file, the file goes to the app you pick in the share sheet. The app itself sends it nowhere.
* **Children.** The app collects no personal data from anyone.
* **Changes.** If the app ever gains a feature that uses the network, this policy will be updated before that release and the change will be stated in the release notes.
